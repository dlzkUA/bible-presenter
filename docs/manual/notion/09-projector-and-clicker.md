# Projector, clearing and the clicker

## Putting things on the projector

- Bible: Show on screen, or double-click a verse in the search results.

- Songs, presentations, media: click a slide.

## Clicker and keyboard

Standard USB presentation clickers and the keyboard both work.

<table header-row="true">
	<tr>
		<td>Key or clicker button</td>
		<td>Action</td>
	</tr>
	<tr>
		<td>→  ↓  Page Down  Space</td>
		<td>Next slide or verse</td>
	</tr>
	<tr>
		<td>←  ↑  Page Up</td>
		<td>Previous slide or verse</td>
	</tr>
	<tr>
		<td>F5</td>
		<td>Bring the last slide back on screen</td>
	</tr>
	<tr>
		<td>Esc</td>
		<td>Clear the screen (with a window open, Esc closes the window instead)</td>
	</tr>
	<tr>
		<td>B, W or period</td>
		<td>Black out the screen, or bring the picture back</td>
	</tr>
</table>

<callout icon="ℹ️" color="blue_bg">
	Keys work while the control window or the projector window is active. While you are typing in a field, the arrows move the cursor and don't change slides.
</callout>

The clicker reads on without stopping: in a song it moves from part to part, in the Bible from the last verse of a chapter to the first verse of the next one, and on into the next book.

No clicker? A phone does the same job and shows what comes next — see Phone remote.
